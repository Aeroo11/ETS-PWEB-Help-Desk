Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common Netflix streaming issues

Why is my Netflix buffering or not loading?

Buffering usually happens because of a slow or unstable internet connection. Here's how to fix it:

  1. Check your internet speed at fast.com (Netflix's own speed test)
  2. Restart your router by unplugging it for 30 seconds
  3. Try a different device if possible
  4. Lower the streaming quality in your Netflix account settings
  5. Make sure no other devices are using significant bandwidth

If these steps don't work, your ISP might be throttling Netflix traffic during peak hours.

How do I fix Netflix error codes?

Common Netflix error codes and solutions:

  • Error UI-800-3 - Clear browser cache or reinstall app
  • Error NW-2-5 - Check network connection
  • Error H7361-1253-80070006 - Restart device
  • Error AVF-FAILED - Update device firmware
  • Error Code: M7111-1331-5059 - You're using a VPN or proxy; turn it off

For any other error codes, try the standard troubleshooting: restart your device, check your internet connection, sign out of Netflix, and sign back in.

Why was my credit card charged twice by Netflix?

Double charges from Netflix can happen for several reasons:

  • You have multiple Netflix accounts using the same payment method
  • Your subscription renewed while a previous payment was still processing
  • You recently upgraded your plan and were charged the difference
  • A family member set up their own account with your card

To resolve this, check your Netflix account page to verify your subscription details, then contact Netflix customer support with your billing date and the amounts charged if you still believe there's an error.

How do I update my payment details on Netflix?

To update your payment method on Netflix:

  1. Log in to your Netflix account
  2. Click on your profile icon in the top right
  3. Select "Account" from the dropdown menu
  4. Under "Membership & Billing" click "Manage payment info"
  5. Select "Add Payment Method" or edit your existing payment details
  6. Enter your new payment information and save changes

If you're unable to update your payment info online, contact Netflix customer support for assistance.

How do I reset my Netflix password?

To reset your Netflix password:

  1. Go to Netflix.com/login
  2. Click the "Need help?" link
  3. Select "Email" or "Text Message" to receive a reset link
  4. Enter your email address or phone number
  5. Check your email or text messages for a reset link
  6. Create a new password that you haven't used before

If you don't receive a reset email or text, check your spam folder or try adding info@netflix.com to your contacts.

How do I manage devices connected to my Netflix account?

To manage devices using your Netflix account:

  1. Sign in to your Netflix account
  2. Go to "Account" from the dropdown menu
  3. Scroll down to "Settings" section
  4. Click "Manage access and devices"
  5. You'll see a list of recent devices that accessed your account
  6. Click "Sign Out" next to any device you want to remove

If you suspect unauthorized access, after signing out all devices, immediately change your password to secure your account.
